# Meta Horizon Store Asset Plan

Updated 2026-10-08. Copy below is a working draft for the fitness-combat position; final copy waits on Phase 6 and 7 features.

## Positioning

**Title:** Phantom Fray: Resonance Rising
**Short pitch:** A boxing workout with a world to save. Punch, block, and dodge the phantoms pouring out of dimensional rifts, and seal the breach before it drains you.
**Category:** Fitness and action. Stationary room-scale, standing, physical dodging, no artificial locomotion.
**Comparable titles for the listing:** Supernatural, Les Mills Bodycombat, Thrill of the Fight. The difference is the stakes: creatures, a life force that drains, Dr. Chen on comms, and a war that needs you back tomorrow.

## Description draft

In 2142 humanity's interdimensional experiments caused The Breach. Conventional weapons pass through the life-force-hungry Phantoms. As a Resonance Strike Force operator you wear Dr. Elara Chen's ERM gauntlets, the only thing that can break a Phantom's link to the Overseer.

Every Phantom is a different movement. Yellow and blue Anglers take a left or right strike on their glowing lure. Green Carapaces crash your chest and need both fists. Pink Spearfins paint a lane and charge it, so step out of the line. Keep your chain alive, keep them out of your head, and seal every rift.

- Stationary room-scale combat, standing, no artificial locomotion
- Four readable Phantom rules across three creature species
- Life force that drains on possession and recovers when you clear the air
- Chain multiplier and resonance crits
- Six-operation campaign with Dr. Chen on comms
- Play your own music from any app while Chen stays on comms
- Replayable hold mode, best scores, and medals (Phase 6)
- A war map where every seal pushes the front line (Phase 7)
