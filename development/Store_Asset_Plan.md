# Meta Horizon Store Asset Plan

## Positioning

**Title:** Phantom Fray: Resonance Rising  
**Short pitch:** Punch, block, and physically dodge ethereal invaders in a focused stationary VR combat mission.

## Description draft

In 2142, humanity's interdimensional experiments caused The Breach. Conventional weapons pass harmlessly through the life-force-hungry Phantoms. As a new Resonance Strike Force operator, you wield Dr. Elara Chen's Ethereal Resonance Matrix gauntlets—the only technology capable of breaking their link to the mysterious Overseer.

Master left- and right-hand resonance strikes, block coordinated attacks, dodge locked phantom charges, protect your life force, and seal three unstable rifts before the invasion window collapses.

- Stationary room-scale combat
- Four readable Phantom combat patterns
- Score chains and resonance-point bonuses
- Short tutorial and replayable 3–5 minute mission
- Standing or seated play with no artificial locomotion
