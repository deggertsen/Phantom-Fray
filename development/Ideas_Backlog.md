# Ideas Backlog

Ideas worth keeping that are not on the roadmap yet. Nothing here is scheduled. When one is picked up, move it to a phase in [Production_Roadmap.md](Production_Roadmap.md). Most of these came from the original concept docs and the pre-October implementation plan; the rest came out of the 2026-10-08 concept review.

## Combat and enemies

- **Shield phantom.** A phantom whose guard breaks only to a specific sequence (a 1-2-3, or hook then uppercut). Teaches combinations instead of single answers.
- **Phantoms that require a specific punch.** Beyond the lure position: a species that only breaks to an uppercut, or only to a hook, so the body is asked for a shape rather than a hand.
- **Ranged drain phantom.** From the lore: a phantom that siphons life force from a distance until it is closed on or disrupted, forcing the player to prioritize it.
- **Projectile phantoms.** Small phantoms or phantom bolts the player punches away, from the original hazard list.
- **Teleporting and area-of-effect phantoms.** From the enemy-system notes: reposition mid-approach, or threaten a cone the player must leave.
- **Phantom evolution.** Phantoms that grow larger and stronger the longer a rift stays open, giving the timer teeth.
- **Boss battles.** Larger phantoms with multiple resonance points struck in sequence, tied to story progression. The Overseer as the final boss: rotating energy rings, crown-like structure, cores targeted in order (see [Phantom_Meshy_Prompts.md](Phantom_Meshy_Prompts.md)).
- **Adaptive AI.** The Overseer learns the player's habits across a session (lead hand, dodge direction) and the mix shifts to punish them. Chen's Gambit already tells this story; the system could do it.
- **Low approaches.** Phantoms that come in at chest or waist height so uppercuts and squats are demanded, not optional.

## Player abilities and pickups

- **Special abilities on cooldown.** The original weapon-system idea: a charged burst, a resonance pulse that clears a ring, or a brief slow.
- **Ability charge from the chain.** Sweet-spot chains charge the special instead of only multiplying score.
- **Power-ups within reach.** Slow time, punch power, life force restore. Grab them physically.
- **ERM reversal.** From the lore: the gauntlets can reverse drain in recently affected victims. A mission beat where the player revives someone, or a mechanic where a clean block restores a sliver of life.

## Hazards and environment

- **Environmental hazards.** Energy beams to duck under, debris to lean away from, floor zones that drain while stood in.
- **Reality distortions.** From the lore: near a strong rift, gravity or scale briefly misbehaves. A presentation beat for later sites.
- **Breach-site variety.** Coastal breach, mountain relay, a city rooftop, the inside of a rift. Scheduled as Phase 8 for the first presets; the rest stays here.

## Modes and structure

- **Simulated training room.** A black-box chamber with damage off, phantom projections, a practice rift, and a species picker. The first operation's training-chamber sign could become literally this.
- **Intelligence-gathering operations.** Capture a specific phantom type, hold a position while Chen's team works, target relay phantoms that are feeding the Overseer.
- **Checkpoints inside long operations.** For 8-minute operations, a mid-operation checkpoint after a seal so a failsafe does not cost the whole run.
- **Earth mission replays and off-planet DLC.** From the original progression notes: replaying cleared breaches with modifiers; a later expansion through a rift to the phantom dimension.
- **Multiplayer.** Co-op holds where two operators share a breach, and the original "life force sharing" idea where one operator can lend the other recovery.
- **Daily and weekly breach.** Scheduled in Phase 7; the seeded, shared version of it with modifiers is still an idea.

## Presentation and audio

- **Aura system.** The original life-force design: a body aura that shifts blue to purple to red, with particle turbulence rising as life force falls. Frost and veins ship today; the aura is the fuller version.
- **Iron Man style HUD.** Arc-reactor life meter, glitch and interference as life force falls, directional damage indicators, proximity alerts.
- **Dynamic music layering.** Stems that add percussion and choir as pressure rises instead of volume and pitch changes (see [Music_Prompts.md](Music_Prompts.md)).
- **Authored creature art.** The procedural species work; a sculpted pass or Meshy-generated models remain an option for a premium trailer.
- **Per-species movement and attack sounds.**

## Platform and community

- **Hand tracking as an input option.** Research gesture recognition for punches without controllers.
- **Achievements.** Flawless seals, maximum chain, all operations gold.
- **Leaderboards.** Scheduled in Phase 7; friend leaderboards and weekly resets stay here.
- **Mod support.** Custom wave tables and breach sites as data files, since operations are already dictionaries.
- **Community features.** Shared war progress (Phase 7), clans or squads, operator profiles.

## Lore and story

- **Expanded storylines.** The RSF's internal rivalries, the cults that worship the phantoms, the governments' scorched-earth response, the ethics of life-force-powered weapons.
- **Story progression through intel.** Each operation reveals one piece of the Overseer's hierarchy; the final confrontation opens a rift into the phantoms' dimension.
- **Chen's arc.** She caused The Breach. A campaign that lets that weigh on her comms over time.
